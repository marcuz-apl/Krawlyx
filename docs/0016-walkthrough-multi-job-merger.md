# Walkthrough 04: Multi-Job Dataset Merger

## Overview
Demonstrates how to merge results from multiple distinct crawl jobs into a unified dataset.

---

## 1. Selecting Jobs to Merge

1. Navigate to the **History** tab (`/admin` or History view).
2. Click the checkboxes next to the crawl jobs you wish to merge (e.g. Job #1, Job #2, Job #3).
3. A sticky action bar appears at the top:
   > **`3 jobs selected`** $\rightarrow$ Click **Merge Selected Datasets →**.

---

## 2. Reviewing & Exporting the Merged Dataset

1. The **Merged Results** page displays all rows from the selected jobs unified into one table.
2. Click **Export Merged CSV** to download a single combined spreadsheet.
3. Click **💾 Save as Dataset** to permanently store the merged table into your SQLite database.
